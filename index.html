Who We Are

RINDADI ENTERPRISE is a premier manufacturing company based in Jos North, Plateau State. Located at No10B Fudawa Village, Bauchi Ring Road, we combine modern production techniques with uncompromising quality standards.

Our facility is equipped to handle diverse manufacturing needs, delivering products that meet both local and international quality benchmarks. We pride ourselves on operational excellence, timely delivery, and building lasting partnerships with our clients.

With deep roots in the Jos community, we understand the unique needs of Nigerian businesses and are committed to supporting industrial growth across the region.
